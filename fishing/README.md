# Fishing: Start Here

> Built 10/11/2026 for family fishing out of Florence, SC (bank, pier, jetty, kayak; no boat right now).
> Everything below was researched against primary sources (SCDNR, SC State Parks, NOAA, SCDES) unless marked otherwise.

## Read these first

1. **You're licensed for freshwater only.** Buy the **$15 annual saltwater license** before any coast trip (not the $25
   Combination; that one doesn't include saltwater). Exception: **Myrtle Beach State Park Pier** has a blanket license,
   so no saltwater license is needed *on that pier*. Kids under 16 need no license, but **every adult does**,
   including the one whose rod you're carrying. → [license.md](license.md)
2. **Red drum rules changed July 1, 2026:** 18–25″ slot, **1 per person, 2 per boat**, and natural bait on 4/0+ hooks must
   be a non-offset, non-stainless **circle hook**. Most fall bull reds must go back. → [regulations.md](regulations.md)
3. **Kids shouldn't eat fish from most Pee Dee-area rivers.** SCDES advises children under 14 to eat *no* fish from any
   water with an advisory, and the Great & Little Pee Dee, Lynches, Black and Waccamaw all have one. Fish those rivers
   catch-and-release. For "we eat what we catch," go salt (flounder, trout, whiting, slot reds; skip king mackerel) or
   Santee Cooper. → [spots.md](spots.md#eating-advisories-scdes-read-before-keeping-freshwater-fish)

Tides shrink through the week (heading toward the first-quarter moon), so early in the week brings stronger current.

## What to throw this fall (short version)

| Fish | Bait | Lure | Kid-rod rig |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whiting / spot / croaker | Shrimp, bloodworms, sand fleas | Fishbites | 2-hook bottom rig, #6 hooks, 1–2 oz |
| Red drum | Cut mullet / menhaden (circle hook!) | Gold spoon, paddletail | Slot reds only; hand bulls to an adult |
| Flounder | Live finger mullet, mud minnows | Gulp! Swimming Mullet | Mud minnow on #2 hook along pilings |
| Seatrout | Live shrimp under a popping cork | MirrOlure, paddletail | Bobber + shrimp |
| Bream (bluegill, redear, redbreast) | Worms, crickets | Beetle Spin, 1/32 oz jig | Bobber + worm, #8 long-shank |
| Crappie | Live minnows | 1/16 oz jig | Bobber + minnow, #4 gold hook |
| Largemouth | Shiners | Lipless crank, spinnerbait, topwater at dawn | Shiner under a bobber |
| Catfish | Nightcrawlers, cut shad, chicken liver | — | Slip-sinker rig, #2 circle hook |

## The tracker app

[apps/catch-log](../apps/catch-log/README.md): logs trips and catches with photo, GPS, time, species, size, weight and
released/kept, with party totals and per-person leaderboards. The main angler is the default; everyone else in the crew is one tap away.
Includes best times (tide movement + dawn/dusk), SC limit warnings, an ID helper, and catch-per-hour insights. Works offline.

**Water temp, live from home:** open any spot in the app with signal to see the live reading from the nearest sensor
(Springmaid Pier, Charleston Harbor, Sampit River, Cooper River, Lake Marion, Moultrie/Wateree tailraces, Little Pee Dee),
the normal for your trip date, and which fish are in their prime range. Normals and the sensor list are in [spots.md](spots.md#water-temperature-sensors--monthly-normals-f).

**No solunar "major/minor" times:** they were removed because the evidence doesn't support them (a 2023 peer-reviewed study
found no link to catch rates). The moon's real effect, bigger or smaller tides, is shown directly from NOAA data.
→ [science.md](science.md)

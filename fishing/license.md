## Kids

All four are under 16, so no SC license is required for SC fresh or salt
waters. Kids still follow the same size and creel limits, and their fish count
toward the boat limit. (Names and ages are kept out of this public repo on purpose.)

## Crossing into NC

An SC license is **not valid in NC waters** (Sunset Beach, Ocean Isle,
Lake Waccamaw, Lumber River above the state line). Buy an NC license for those trips.

## Checklist before any trip

- [ ] License pulled up in the Go Outdoors SC app (or a screenshot saved)
- [ ] Saltwater license active (coast trips)
- [ ] Ruler or measuring board in the pack (flounder, red drum, trout are all size-limited)
- [ ] Regs checked for the target species → see [regulations.md](regulations.md)

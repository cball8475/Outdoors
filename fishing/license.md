**Coverage gap:** Freshwater only. Do not fish piers, surf, jetties, inlets, or
Winyah Bay until the saltwater license is bought. Plan: buy it before the first
coast trip.

**What to buy:** Resident **Annual Saltwater** license, **$15** (or 3-year, $45;
same price per year, one less renewal). Verified on dnr.sc.gov 10/11/2026.

**Don't buy a Combination ($25) or Sportsman ($50) license for this.** Neither
one includes saltwater. They cover hunting, big game, and *freshwater* fishing
only. Saltwater is always a separate purchase (or a lifetime add-on).

**Renewal dates:** Saltwater bought in October renews in October; freshwater
renews in April. Two dates, $25/yr total. Turn on auto-renew for the saltwater
license too so neither one lapses.

**When you don't need the saltwater license** (verified, SCDNR):
- Fishing from a **licensed public pier** that carries a blanket license (e.g.
  Myrtle Beach State Park pier). This does *not* cover jetties, surf, docks, or
  the MarshWalk.
- Fishing on a licensed **charter / headboat**.
- Using 3 or fewer drop nets, traps, or hookless handlines (crabbing with kids).

## Lifetime licenses: worth it? (prices verified dnr.sc.gov 10/11/2026)

**SC resident lifetime prices**

| Age | Option | Price | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16–63 | Lifetime freshwater fishing | $300 | Freshwater |
| 16–63 | Lifetime saltwater fishing | $300 | Saltwater |
| 16–63 | Lifetime combination | $500 | Hunting + big game + freshwater |
| 16–63 | Saltwater add-on to lifetime combination | $200 | Saltwater |
| 2–15 | Lifetime combination | $400 | Hunting + big game + freshwater |
| 2–15 | Saltwater add-on | $160 | Saltwater |
| 64+ | **Senior fishing license** | **$9** | Freshwater **and** saltwater, for life |

Requires 180 days of SC residency, based on the issue date of your SCDMV license. The page shows no effective date
for lifetime prices; other items on it expire 6/30/2027, so April 2027 should be the same price schedule (not guaranteed).

**Adult (16–63): fresh + salt lifetime = $600.** It only has to cover you until 64, when the $9 senior
license covers both. Annual fresh + salt = $25/yr.

| Your age now | Years to 64 | Annual, flat prices | Annual, fees +3%/yr | Today's value of annual (5% return, +3% fees) | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 35 | 29 | $725 | $1,130 | $561 | $600 |
| 38 | 26 | $650 | $964 | $516 | $600 |
| 40 | 24 | $600 | $861 | $485 | $600 |
| 45 | 19 | $475 | $628 | $402 | $600 |

**Verdict:** for an adult in their mid-30s or older, lifetime fresh + salt costs about the same as or more than paying yearly once you count
what $600 earns elsewhere. Buy it for convenience or as a hedge against fee hikes, not to save money. 3-year
licenses ($30 + $45) give the "renew less often" benefit at the same $25/yr.

**If you buy lifetime anyway:** buy the **lifetime saltwater now** instead of the $15 annual (no
6-month overlap), buy **lifetime freshwater in April**, and **turn OFF auto-renew** on the annual
freshwater license before 4/21/2027.

**Kids:** under 16, lifetime is only sold as a Combination ($400 + $160 saltwater = $560/kid, includes
hunting). The price jumps at 16 ($600 fishing-only or $700 combo + salt). Worth revisiting before the
oldest turns 16 if the kids stick with fishing and/or hunting.

## Crossing into NC

An SC license is **not valid in NC waters** (Sunset Beach, Ocean Isle,
Lake Waccamaw, Lumber River above the state line). Buy an NC license for those trips.
